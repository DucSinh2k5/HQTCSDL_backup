1. TONG QUAN BAI TOAN VA MUC TIEU

Muc tieu cua de tai la xay dung mot he thong hoc may de phan loai tin hieu giao dich chung khoan thanh 3 nhom:

- SELL: tin hieu ban hoac can giam rui ro.
- HOLD: tin hieu nam giu, chua co loi the ro rang.
- BUY: tin hieu mua khi mo hinh nhan thay xac suat tang gia cao hon.

Bai toan duoc thiet ke duoi dang phan loai da lop. Dau vao la du lieu gia, khoi luong va cac chi bao ky thuat cua tung ma co phieu theo tung ngay giao dich. Dau ra la tin hieu SELL / HOLD / BUY.

De phu hop voi bai toan giao dich, he thong khong chi danh gia mo hinh bang Accuracy ma con danh gia chat luong tin hieu hanh dong:

- Accuracy: do dung tong the tren ca 3 lop SELL / HOLD / BUY.
- BUY Precision: trong cac tin hieu BUY, co bao nhieu tin hieu dung la BUY.
- SELL Precision: trong cac tin hieu SELL, co bao nhieu tin hieu dung la SELL.
- Backtest metrics: loi nhuan, Sharpe, hit rate va chi phi giao dich.

Ket qua tong quan sau khi chay tu ClickHouse:

- Accuracy: 46.69%
- Baseline Accuracy: 38.63%
- Macro F1-score: 0.4433
- BUY Precision goc: 45.59%
- SELL Precision goc: 47.09%
- BUY Precision sau loc tin hieu chat: 69.58%
- SELL Precision sau loc tin hieu chat: 66.21%
- So tin hieu hanh dong sau loc: 8,592 dong

Mo hinh co them lop loc tin hieu dua tren xac suat du doan. Thay vi lay moi du doan BUY / SELL cua model, he thong chi giu tin hieu hanh dong khi model co do tu tin cao.

Nguong tin hieu hien tai:

- SIGNAL_MIN_ACTION_PROBABILITY = 0.60
- SIGNAL_MIN_ACTION_MARGIN = 0.00

Y nghia:

- BUY chi duoc chap nhan khi P(BUY) >= 60% va P(BUY) >= P(SELL).
- SELL chi duoc chap nhan khi P(SELL) >= 60% va P(SELL) >= P(BUY).
- Cac truong hop con lai duoc dua ve HOLD de tranh tin hieu yeu hoac model con phan van.

Viec siet nguong lam so luong tin hieu BUY / SELL giam xuong, nhung precision cua cac tin hieu hanh dong tang len ro ret. Day la cach phu hop hon voi ung dung giao dich, vi tin hieu it nhung dang tin cay thuong co gia tri hon tin hieu nhieu nhung nhieu nhieu.

3. KIEN TRUC MO HINH VA THUAT TOAN

Mo hinh chinh duoc su dung la XGBoost Classifier.

XGBoost la thuat toan gradient boosting dua tren cay quyet dinh, phu hop voi du lieu dang bang va cac feature ky thuat. Mo hinh co kha nang hoc quan he phi tuyen, ket hop nhieu cay quyet dinh yeu thanh mo hinh manh hon, dong thoi cung cap feature importance de giai thich ket qua.

Bai toan duoc cau hinh thanh phan loai 3 lop:

- 0: SELL
- 1: HOLD
- 2: BUY

Tham so mo hinh:

- n_estimators: 1000
- learning_rate: 0.02
- max_depth: 4
- min_child_weight: 3
- subsample: 0.8
- colsample_bytree: 0.8
- reg_alpha: 0.1
- reg_lambda: 1.0
- objective: multi:softprob
- eval_metric: mlogloss
- num_class: 3
- random_state: 42
- n_jobs: -1

Dau ra cua XGBoost gom nhan du doan va xac suat cho tung lop:

- sell_probability
- hold_probability
- buy_probability

He thong su dung cac xac suat nay de tao them tin hieu sau loc:

- predicted_signal: nhan goc cua model.
- adjusted_signal: tin hieu sau khi loc theo xac suat va margin.

Khac biet giua prob va margin:

- prob la xac suat model gan cho mot tin hieu. Vi du P(BUY) = 0.60 nghia la model tin BUY voi muc 60%.
- margin la do chenh giua BUY va SELL. Cong thuc cho tin hieu BUY la margin = P(BUY) - P(SELL).

Trong cau hinh hien tai, he thong dung nguong P >= 60%. Muc nay giup loai bo cac tin hieu hanh dong yeu va giu lai cac tin hieu co do tu tin cao hon.

6. HAN CHE VA HUONG CAI TIEN

Han che:

- Accuracy tong the chua cao, model moi dat muc trung binh neu xet theo bai toan phan loai 3 lop.
- Mo hinh con nham lan dang ke giua BUY va SELL.
- Lop HOLD kho phan biet vi bien dong nho quanh nguong -1% den +1%.
- Backtest voi nguong BUY 60% co qua it ngay giao dich va loi nhuan sau phi dang am.
- Hit Rate trong backtest con thap.
- Du lieu chu yeu la gia, khoi luong va chi bao ky thuat, chua co tin tuc, bao cao tai chinh, dong tien nganh hay chi so thi truong chung.
- Cac nguong hien tai duoc chon dua tren du lieu da co, can can than de tranh overfitting khi ap dung vao tuong lai.

Huong cai tien:

- Toi uu nguong BUY / SELL bang walk-forward validation thay vi chi xem tren mot tap test co dinh.
- Thu nghiem cac nguong nhu P >= 0.55, P >= 0.60, P >= 0.65 de can bang giua precision va so luong tin hieu.
- Tach 2 muc nguong: nguong dashboard de hien thi tin hieu chat, va nguong backtest de toi uu loi nhuan sau phi.
- Them feature ky thuat nhu RSI, MACD, Bollinger Band, ADX, ATR va momentum theo nganh.
- Them feature ve thi truong chung nhu VNINDEX return, trend, volatility va thanh khoan toan thi truong.
- Loc bo co phieu thanh khoan qua thap hoac co du lieu bat thuong.
- Thu nghiem model rieng cho BUY va SELL thay vi chi dung mot model 3 lop.
- Thu nghiem LightGBM, CatBoost hoac ensemble de so sanh voi XGBoost.
- Toi uu chien luoc backtest theo tan suat giao dich, chi phi, stop-loss, take-profit va ty trong danh muc.
- Kiem tra do on dinh cua mo hinh qua nhieu giai doan thi truong khac nhau.
- Them bao cao risk management: maximum drawdown, turnover, average holding return, win/loss ratio.

Ket luan:

Model 3 da hoan thanh pipeline tu du lieu ClickHouse, feature engineering, huan luyen, danh gia, backtest den dashboard bao cao. Mo hinh goc co Accuracy 46.69%, cao hon baseline 38.63%, cho thay co hoc duoc tin hieu tu du lieu lich su.

Neu su dung tat ca du doan goc, precision cua BUY va SELL con thap. Khi them lop loc xac suat voi nguong P >= 60%, BUY Precision tang len 69.58% va SELL Precision tang len 66.21%. Dieu nay phu hop voi muc tieu hien thi tin hieu giao dich co do tin cay cao hon.

Tuy nhien, backtest voi nguong chat hien tai chua tao loi nhuan rong sau chi phi. Do do, mo hinh hien tai phu hop lam nen tang nghien cuu, dashboard va bao cao phan tich. Truoc khi ap dung vao giao dich thuc te, can tiep tuc toi uu feature, nguong tin hieu, chien luoc backtest va danh gia rui ro day du hon.

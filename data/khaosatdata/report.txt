REPORT: y nghia cac file trong thu muc khaosatdata

Muc tieu
- Cac file nay duoc sinh ra tu viec khao sat du lieu Data_500_stocks_dirty.csv.
- Moi file la mot nhom kiem tra rieng, giup phat hien loi, thong ke, va cac diem bat thuong.

1) File tong hop
- khaosat_summary.txt
  + Tom tat nhanh ket qua: so dong/cot, kieu du lieu, so loi theo tung nhom.
  + Huu ich khi can biet nhanh tinh trang toan bo du lieu.

2) Thieu du lieu (missing)
- missing_summary.csv
  + So luong va ty le gia tri thieu theo tung cot.
  + Cot mau: column, missing_count, missing_ratio.
  + Vi du: column=volume, missing_count=120 nghia la co 120 dong volume bi thieu.

- rows_with_missing.csv
  + Tat ca dong co it nhat mot gia tri thieu.
  + Dung de xem nhung dong can bo sung hoac loai bo.

3) Trung lap (duplicate)
- duplicate_full_rows.csv
  + Cac dong bi trung hoan toan (tat ca cot giong nhau).
  + Vi du: 2 dong co cung time, open, high, low, close, volume, symbol.

- duplicate_symbol_time.csv
  + Cac dong bi trung theo khoa symbol + time.
  + Vi du: cung symbol=ABR va cung time=2018-07-02 07:00:00 xuat hien nhieu lan.

4) Kiem tra symbol
- symbol_counts.csv
  + Thong ke so dong theo moi symbol.
  + Cot mau: symbol, row_count.

- invalid_symbol.csv
  + Cac dong co symbol sai dinh dang.
  + Tieu chuan: chi chap nhan ky tu A-Z va 0-9, khong dau cach.
  + Vi du khong hop le: 'aBr', 'VIC ' (co dau cach), 'VN-INDEX'.

5) Kiem tra gia (OHLC)
- non_positive_prices.csv
  + Dong co gia open/high/low/close <= 0.
  + Vi du: open=0 hoac close=-1.

- invalid_ohlc_rows.csv
  + Dong vi pham quy tac OHLC.
  + Quy tac: high >= open/close/low va low <= open/close.
  + Vi du khong hop le: high < low, hoac low > close.

6) Kiem tra volume
- negative_volume.csv
  + Dong co volume am (volume < 0).
  + Vi du: volume = -100.

7) Thong ke mo ta
- numeric_stats.csv
  + Thong ke cho cac cot so: open, high, low, close, volume.
  + Cot mau: column, count, mean, std, min, 25%, 50%, 75%, max.
  + Dung de nhan biet quy mo va do phan tan du lieu.

8) Outlier (IQR)
- outlier_summary.csv
  + Tom tat nguong outlier theo IQR cho tung cot so.
  + Cot mau: column, q1, q3, iqr, lower, upper, outlier_count.
  + Luu y: outlier chi la bat thuong theo thong ke, khong chac la loi.

- outliers_open.csv / outliers_high.csv / outliers_low.csv / outliers_close.csv / outliers_volume.csv
  + Cac dong co gia tri outlier cho tung cot tuong ung.
  + Cot mau thuong gom: time, symbol, <cot dang xet>.

Goi y xu ly
- Uu tien kiem tra duplicate_symbol_time.csv va invalid_ohlc_rows.csv.
- Doi voi outlier, nen kiem tra theo tung symbol hoac theo khoang thoi gian.
- Neu can bo sung them kiem tra (vi du: time khong dung dinh dang, volume khong nguyen), hay bao minh.
